# Independent Penetration Test Brief

## Scope
- Domain produksi, Netlify Functions, autentikasi, dashboard seluruh peran, Supabase/RLS/storage, webhook pembayaran, upload, privacy export/deletion, dan support tickets.
- Uji OWASP ASVS/WSTG yang relevan: auth/session, authorization/IDOR, injection, XSS/CSRF, SSRF, file upload, rate limits, secrets, business logic, dan dependency risk.

## Rules
- Wajib tertulis: jendela tes, IP sumber, kontak darurat, batas destructive testing, dan penanganan data.
- Temuan Critical/High harus ditutup dan diuji ulang sebelum launch.
