# Data Retention Schedule

This schedule must be approved by the operator and legal adviser. Suggested defaults:

- Account/profile: account lifetime plus 30 days after verified deletion request.
- Learning attempts and feedback: account lifetime or contractual program period.
- Payment records: statutory accounting period applicable to the legal entity.
- Support tickets: 24 months after closure.
- Audit/security logs: 12 months, longer only when required for an investigation.
- Unsubmitted recordings/uploads: automatically purge after 30 days.
- Backups: encrypted rolling retention with tested expiry and restore procedures.

Deletion must propagate to storage, derived data, vendor systems, and backups according to documented exceptions.
