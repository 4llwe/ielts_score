# Incident Response Runbook

1. **Detect and classify** — security, privacy, availability, payment, or academic-integrity incident.
2. **Contain** — revoke sessions, rotate keys, disable affected feature, preserve logs.
3. **Assess** — affected users, records, time window, financial impact, and legal notification duties.
4. **Eradicate and recover** — patch, restore from verified backup, reconcile transactions, validate RLS.
5. **Communicate** — one accountable incident lead; factual status updates; no unsupported promises.
6. **Review** — root cause, corrective actions, owners, deadlines, and follow-up verification.

Production launch requires named on-call contacts, severity definitions, communication templates, vendor escalation paths, and a completed tabletop exercise.
