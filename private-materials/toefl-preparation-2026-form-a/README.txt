Designed for a 90-minute classroom session plus speaking administration. All questions are original and are not official ETS items. The package covers every task family listed by ETS for the TOEFL iBT format after 21 January 2026, but Reading and Listening are shortened and non-adaptive. Classroom scores must not be reported as official TOEFL scores.
